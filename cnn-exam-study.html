Everything You Need

Built for how nurses actually study

Whether you have ten minutes between shifts or a full evening, the app adapts to your schedule and your learning style.

📝

Practice Questions

1315 questions across the twelve core topics, each with a clear explanation. Questions shuffle every time so you truly learn the material.

💡

Explain More

Every question carries a second, deeper explanation behind an Explain More button. Tap it when the short answer isn't enough and get the full reasoning, plus why each of the other choices was wrong. Instant, offline, and yours to open anytime.

🃏

Smart Flashcards

753 flashcards with spaced repetition — cards you miss come back sooner, so you master them faster.

📚

Study Lessons

144 concise lessons with diagrams that teach key concepts in plain language — twelve for each of the twelve core topics.

🎯

Focus Suggestions

The app analyzes your wrong answers and tells you exactly which topics to study next.

⏱️

80-Hour Goal Tracker

A visual progress bar toward your 80-hour study goal — with milestones to keep you motivated, plus a daily-goal ring: pick 15, 30, 60, or 90 minutes a day and watch today's ring fill.

🔊

Read-Aloud Mode

Listen to lessons and flashcards hands-free. New Auto-Play runs the whole lesson list continuously — one tap, then it reads lesson after lesson and loops back on its own. Built for the drive in.

🔍

AI Evaluate

On the Trackers screen, get a personalized, in-depth assessment of your study metrics — strengths, weak topics, and a step-by-step plan to pass.

⚡

Weak-Topic Drill

One tap on the Practice screen auto-builds a 15-question set from your lowest-accuracy topics, so you drill exactly what you keep missing.

🧩

Distinct Exam Versions

Three full 150-question simulations, each drawing its own question set from the whole bank — still weighted to the official exam blueprint.

📊

Blueprint Readiness

After each practice exam, Trackers charts your score on all 7 official exam objectives and shows whether each one is climbing or slipping between attempts — so you can see which objective is capping your score.

📘

Built-In How-To Guide

A complete walkthrough lives inside the app: setup, screen-by-screen guidance, and study tips, one tap from the sign-in screen. Nothing to download, and it works on any phone.

🧠

Test-Taking Strategies

23 skimmable strategies in four categories — pacing yourself, decoding question stems, narrowing answer choices, and handling exam-day nerves.

🔤

Abbreviation Glossary

All 57 nephrology abbreviations — AKI, CRRT, KDPI, TMP and the rest — are spelled out in full the first time they appear in a question, an answer choice, or an explanation, so nothing is left as a bare acronym.

✅

Wrong-Answer Review

Miss a question and you see exactly what you picked beside the correct answer, with the explanation written in plain language — so a wrong answer teaches you instead of just costing you a point.

🏅

Live Score & Rating

A running score sits under every practice question — how many you've answered, the percentage you're getting right, and a plain-language read (Strong, On track, Building, Early stage) — so you know where you stand mid-quiz instead of waiting for the final total.
