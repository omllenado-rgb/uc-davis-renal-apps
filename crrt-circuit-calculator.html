📋 Circuit lifespan — what to use

  • 48 h — the common default when a change is due at 48 hours, provided the circuit is still running well
  • 72 h — typical maximum on a well-functioning circuit per KDIGO / manufacturer guidance
  • Up to 96 h — some units and device makers allow it when pressures, access and filter performance stay stable
  • Change sooner — for clotted filter, rising transmembrane pressure, declining effluent rate, or suspected infection
  • Always follow your unit protocol and the ordering provider
