📋 Circuit lifespan — what to use

  • 48 h — the common default change time on a circuit that is still running well
  • 72 h — typical maximum per KDIGO / manufacturer guidance
  • Up to 96 h — where unit protocol and device maker allow it, with stable pressures
  • Change sooner — clotted filter, rising transmembrane pressure, falling effluent rate, or suspected infection
  • The machine's countdown is the authoritative reference for the prescribed lifespan
