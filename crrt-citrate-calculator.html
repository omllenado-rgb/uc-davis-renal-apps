Lab draw schedule

  • System sample — post-filter port of the CRRT circuit
  • Patient sample — peripheral IV or central line
  • 30 min — after treatment initiation, blood transfusion, or termination
  • Hourly — after any ACD-A or calcium change, until stable for 2 consecutive draws
  • Every 4 hours — once stable and "no change"
  • PRN — as needed
