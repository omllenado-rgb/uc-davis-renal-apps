Drug Dialyzability Reference

Conventional HD • High-Flux HD • CRRT

Updated: August 2026 | 200+ Drugs | New & International Medications

✅ ALWAYS GIVE POST-HEMODIALYSIS

  • Aminoglycosides (Gentamicin, Tobramycin)
  • Vancomycin, Daptomycin
  • Beta-lactams (Cefepime, Meropenem, Pip-Tazo)
  • Gabapentin, Pregabalin, Levetiracetam
  • Acyclovir, Valacyclovir
  • Atenolol, Sotalol, Lisinopril
  • Sitagliptin, Fluconazole

❌ NEVER SUPPLEMENT

  • Warfarin (99% protein bound)
  • Phenytoin, Valproic Acid
  • Digoxin (large Vd)
  • Amiodarone, SSRIs, TCAs
  • Statins, Benzodiazepines
  • Insulin, monoclonal antibodies

⚠️ CONTRAINDICATED IN ESRD

  • Metformin (lactic acidosis)
  • Lithium (toxicity)
  • Dabigatran (accumulation)
  • Morphine (metabolite toxicity)
  • NSAIDs (bleeding, renal)
  • Sulfonylureas (hypoglycemia)
