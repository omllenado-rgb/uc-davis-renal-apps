Peritoneal Dialysis Adequacy (Weekly)

📋 How to Collect (24-Hour Collection)

  1. Dialysate: Collect ALL drained dialysate over 24 hours. Mix thoroughly, then take a small sample for urea measurement. Record the total drain volume (L).
  2. Urine: Collect ALL urine over the same 24 hours (if the patient still makes urine). Record volume and take a sample for urea.
  3. Blood: Draw a serum BUN at the midpoint of the collection period.
  4. Send: Label all samples (dialysate urea, urine urea, serum BUN) and send to lab.

📋 KDOQI Guidelines — PD Adequacy

  • Weekly Kt/V: ≥ 1.7 (target)
  • Peritoneal Kt/V: contributes most of the clearance
  • Renal Kt/V: residual kidney function counts — preserve it
  • Below 1.7: consider increasing dwell volume, exchanges, or cycler time
